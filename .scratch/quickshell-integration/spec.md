# Broader QuickShell integration

Decide whether Shaula should expand beyond its Noctalia-specific integration,
then deliver portable QuickShell support incrementally without weakening the
existing capture-session or panel-hide contracts.

Decision: deferred on 2026-08-12 because no concrete non-Noctalia consumer
currently justifies the expansion. The existing Noctalia integration remains in
place; the implementation tickets are `wontfix` unless that product gate is
reopened.

Planning source: `docs/plan-quickshell-integration.md`. That document remains a
draft until the first ticket resolves its architecture and user-value questions.
