## What to build

Choose one concrete user problem not solved by the current Undo and Redo
controls, validate its interaction in Preview, and deliver the smallest visible
history affordance that solves it without exposing internal snapshot machinery.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The target user problem and rejected alternatives are documented before implementation.
- [ ] The proposed interaction is reviewed in the real Preview UI.
- [ ] The affordance stays synchronized with undo/redo availability and document mutations.
- [ ] Existing keyboard shortcuts and history bounds remain intact.
